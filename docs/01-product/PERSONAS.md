# Personas

## 1. SEO Agency (Primary Launch Segment)

**Who:** Digital marketing agencies managing SEO and content for 10–100 clients.

**Pain:** AI search disrupts their core business. Clients ask "why am I not in ChatGPT?"
and they have no answer. Their existing tools (SEMrush, Ahrefs) don't cover AI visibility.

**Goal:** Show measurable AI visibility results to clients. Justify retainer fees in the AI era.

**Budget:** $500–$2000/month/agency (across all client accounts)

**Decision maker:** Agency founder or Head of SEO

---

## 2. Growing Business (Core Product-Market Fit Target)

**Who:** Companies with $1M–$50M revenue, 1–3 people in marketing, no dedicated SEO team.

**Pain:** They know they need to "be found online" but don't have the expertise or time
to figure out AI-era visibility. They feel left behind.

**Goal:** Stop worrying about digital visibility. Have someone (or something) handle it.

**Budget:** $200–$800/month

**Decision maker:** Marketing Manager, CMO, or Founder

---

## 3. Enterprise Marketing (Phase 2)

**Who:** Marketing teams at $50M+ companies with dedicated digital/SEO/content functions.

**Pain:** Fragmented tools, no single source of truth for digital presence, can't measure
ROI of visibility investments across channels.

**Goal:** Unified visibility intelligence. Proof of impact for exec reporting.

**Budget:** $2000–$10000/month

**Decision maker:** VP Marketing, Head of Digital, CMO
