# PRD — Product Requirements Document

## Problem

Businesses cannot consistently manage their visibility across search and AI.

Traditional SEO tools track rankings. They don't tell you what to do.
Knowledge management tools store data. They don't act on it.
AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) changed how businesses get discovered —
and no tool closes the full loop from knowledge to action to outcome.

## Solution

Atlas — the Company Brain.

A system that:
1. Builds and maintains a verified Knowledge Graph of your business
2. Collects visibility signals across search, AI, entities, and reputation
3. Decides which actions will have the highest impact today
4. Executes approved actions safely
5. Measures outcomes and learns

## Target Segments (Priority Order)

1. **SEO Agencies** — primary launch segment. They have clients, budgets, and feel the
   AI-search disruption directly. One agency = 20-100 client companies.

2. **Growing Business** — companies $1M–$50M revenue with a marketing function but
   no enterprise tooling budget. Underserved by Yext (enterprise-only).

3. **Enterprise Marketing** — second phase, after Product-Market Fit with segment 1-2.

## MVP Features

1. **Knowledge Ingestion** — load and structure business knowledge into the Company Brain
2. **Visibility Audit** — measure how the business is seen across search, AI, entity, reputation
3. **Recommendations** — prioritized action list with expected impact, confidence, and explanation

## Success Metrics

- Time to first Visibility Audit: < 10 minutes after onboarding
- Recommendation acceptance rate: > 60%
- Visibility Score improvement after 30 days: measurable in 80% of accounts
