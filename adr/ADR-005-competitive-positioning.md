# ADR-005: Competitive Positioning

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-05

## Context

After studying Yext (the most structurally similar competitor), we need to formally
define how OmniIQ is differentiated and what category we are creating.

## Yext Analysis

Yext is an Enterprise Agentic Marketing Platform centered on a Knowledge Graph.
They sync business data across search engines, AI, maps, and directories.

**What Yext does:**
- Stores structured business data (Knowledge Graph)
- Distributes that data to 200+ platforms
- Enterprise-focused: chains, banks, medical networks, franchises

**What Yext does NOT do:**
- Decide which actions will have the highest impact
- Measure ROI of each action
- Build a learning loop (action → outcome → improved recommendations)
- Serve growing businesses ($1M-$50M) affordably
- Explain the reasoning behind recommendations

## Decision

### Category We Create: Autonomous Visibility Management (AVM)

We are not a Knowledge Management tool. We are not an SEO tool.
OmniIQ is the first Autonomous Visibility Management platform.

### Positioning

```
Yext knows who you are.
OmniIQ knows what you should do.
```

### The Closed Loop (Our Core Differentiator)

```
Company Brain → Signal Collection → Gap Analysis → Decision Engine
      ↑                                                      ↓
Learning ←── Revenue Attribution ←── Outcome ←── Execution (approved)
```

No competitor closes this loop today. This is our moat.

### Our Moat Strategy

**Primary moat: Switching Cost + Data**
- A company's Knowledge Graph built over 12 months is expensive to recreate
- The Learning Engine improves with data — accuracy compounds over time
- Action history + attribution creates institutional memory that cannot be exported meaningfully

**Secondary moat: Category Ownership**
- OmniIQ owns the term "Autonomous Visibility Management"
- Publish research, benchmark data, and thought leadership on AI visibility
- The "Entity Authority Score" metric becomes industry standard

## Consequences

- Marketing must never use the words "Knowledge Graph" in external comms (too close to Yext)
- Use: "Company Brain", "Visibility Intelligence", "Autonomous Visibility"
- Pricing must be accessible to Growing Business — not enterprise-only
- Decision Engine and Learning Engine are non-negotiable MVP features (not post-MVP)
